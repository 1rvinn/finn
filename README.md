# finn.

don't you always feel that your monthly allowances fly by real quick, despite you having barely spent on anything? \
worry no more, use finn. and get to have a closer look at your money \

- integrable with your notion page so you cann access your transaction info easily
- a chatbot to answer all your questions and give suggestions to help you manage your money better
- all this happens locally so no need to fret about your data privacy!
